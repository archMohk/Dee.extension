BatchPack

DeeCloseAll

Close currently open Revit documents — choose Synchronize & Close, Relinquish & Close, or Close without Relinquish.

Risk level: High (for one of its 3 modes) "Relinquish & Close" discards any unsynced local changes in the selected documents. The tool does warn you in its confirmation dialog, but double-check you picked the right mode before confirming.

What it does

Lists every currently open, non-linked Revit document as a checklist, and applies one of three closing modes to everything you check:

  • Synchronize & Close — saves local changes, runs a full Synchronize With Central, relinquishes worksets/permissions, then closes.
  • Relinquish & Close (unsaved changes lost) — releases your worksets/borrowed elements without pushing local changes to central, then closes. Any unsynced local edits are lost.
  • Close without Relinquish — just closes; your worksets/borrowed elements stay checked out under your name until you deal with it or reopen the file.

How to use it

  1. Check which open documents to act on.
  2. Pick one of the 3 modes.
  3. Confirm in the dialog (it restates the mode and, for Relinquish, warns that unsynced changes will be lost).
  4. Review the results report — the currently active document's close is deferred and finishes right after the tool exits.

Before you run it

  • Only pick Relinquish & Close if you're certain you don't need whatever local changes are in those documents.
  • Synchronize & Close pushes to the shared central model, which is visible to every collaborator on that project.
