How to use

Touch — one finger drags to orbit, two fingers pinch to zoom and slide to pan, a single tap selects an element.

Mouse — left button orbits, right button or middle button pans, the wheel zooms towards the pointer, a click selects.

Walk through it — W forward, S back, A left, D right, Q down, E up. Hold Shift to move faster. Arrow keys work too, and there is a Move speed slider in the menu.

Menu (☰) — search elements, switch whole categories on and off, and cut the model open with the section box.

Select an element to read its category, family, type and level, then Isolate it or Hide it.

Measure / Angle — tap points on the model. It snaps to corners, and shows the straight distance plus the X, Y and Z differences.

Comment — tap a spot to pin a note to it. Notes and saved viewpoints are kept in this browser; use Copy all in the menu to send them to someone else.

View (in the menu) — switch between perspective and isometric, set the lens angle and the exact turn and height, choose the measuring units, and make the whole model or single categories see-through. The keyplan in the corner shows where you are looking from.

Reset all puts every category, every hidden element and the section box back to how the file opened.
